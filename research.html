Research

My research intertwines philosophy of physics, general philosophy of science, and metaphysics of science. I am currently mainly focused on three research areas.

[1] Inter-level relations in science

My main academic interest is how scientific theories that describe reality at different levels are related, and in particular the conditions under which one physical theory can be reduced to another. I addressed reduction across a range of contexts, including thermodynamics, quantum mechanics, astrophysics, and biochemistry. A central focus of this work has been the development of functional reduction within the philosophy of science [SEP]. I am also interested in effective realism.

[2] Metaphysics of Spacetime

I work on the ontology of spacetime, in both classical and relativistic physics. My research focuses especially on tense realism and the relationship between space, time, and spacetime, with particular attention to what physics tells us about simultaneity and the unification of space and time into spacetime.

[3] Philosophy of Thermodynamics

I'm interested in the foundations of thermodynamics and statistical mechanics, both at the classical and quantum levels. I'm especially interested in the relationship between these theories and in their applicability to unconventional domains, such as self-gravitating systems like globular clusters.
